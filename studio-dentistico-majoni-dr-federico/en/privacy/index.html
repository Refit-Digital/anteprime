Privacy policy

Data controller

Dr. Federico Majoni, Majoni Dental Practice (Studio Dentistico Majoni Dr. Federico), Via Roma 6, 32043 Cortina d'Ampezzo (BL), Italy. Email: studiomajoni@gmail.com, phone +39 0436 866030.

Data you send with the contact form

Your name, phone number, email address (optional) and your message: only what you type into the form.

We use it only to reply to you, for example to arrange an appointment. Legal basis: your consent and steps taken at your request before any treatment (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a) and (b)). We don't use your data for marketing and we don't pass it on to anyone else.

Your name and phone number are needed so that we can call you back: without them we can't reply. Email and message are optional.

Please don't send medical details

Please don't write anything about your health in the form. We'll discuss that in person or by phone.

Who receives the data

Web3forms (Web3Creative) delivers the form as our data processor and forwards your message to the practice's email inbox. According to its privacy policy, Web3forms operates from India and uses providers (Amazon Web Services, Cloudflare, Hetzner) that may process data in several regions, including outside the EU. Transfers from the European Economic Area are made under the Standard Contractual Clauses in its data processing agreement. To filter spam, the sender's IP address and email may be checked by the CleanTalk and Akismet services. Web3forms keeps submissions for up to three years unless they are deleted earlier.

Google provides the practice's mailbox (Gmail), so messages we receive are stored on Google's servers, which may process them outside the EU.

Browsing data

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. (United States). As GitHub's Pages documentation states, GitHub logs and stores each visitor's IP address for security purposes. The practice doesn't use this data and has no access to these logs. Legal basis: legitimate interest in keeping the site secure (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)). More in GitHub's privacy statement.

How long we keep it

We keep messages in the practice's mailbox only as long as needed to reply and handle any appointment, then delete them. For the copy kept by Web3forms, see above.

Cookies

This site doesn't use profiling cookies or analytics cookies, and it loads no third-party tracking tools: the fonts are hosted on the site itself, maps open on the provider's own site only when you tap the link, and there are no analytics.

The contact form sends your data to Web3forms, which doesn't set cookies on this site.

The browser's local storage (localStorage) is used only for technical purposes, with these keys:

  • mj-cookie-notice = 1: remembers that you closed the cookie notice.
  • mj-consent = accepted / rejected: used only if optional third-party services are added in the future; it records your choice.

Both stay until you clear the site's data in your browser. They contain no personal data and are never sent to any server.

To change your choice, use the Cookie preferences link at the bottom of the page, or clear the site's data in your browser.

Legal basis: art. 122 of the Italian Privacy Code and the Garante's cookie guidelines of 10 June 2021 (technical tools don't need consent).

Your rights

You can ask at any time to see, correct or delete your data, restrict or object to its use, or receive it in a common format (data portability), and you can withdraw your consent, by emailing studiomajoni@gmail.com. Withdrawing consent doesn't affect what was done before. You can also complain to the Italian data protection authority, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali (www.garanteprivacy.it).

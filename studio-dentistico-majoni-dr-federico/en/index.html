Dental emergencies

Toothache on holiday? Sudden pain, a broken tooth, a lost crown or filling: please call +39 0436 866030 first. We'll tell you whether and when we can see you. The practice works by appointment only, so please don't come in without calling ahead.

About

Majoni Dental Practice is at Via Roma 6, in Cortina d'Ampezzo. Dr. Federico Majoni sees patients for everything from check-ups and cleaning to implants, crowns and dentures.

The practice works by appointment only. To book, call +39 0436 866030 or send us a message using the form below.
